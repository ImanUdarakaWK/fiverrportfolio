02 — About

A studio of one, building patiently.

I'm Iman, an independent designer based in Sri Lanka. I design logos and brand identities for people building small things they care about.

I started designing in 2023 — self-taught at first, then through structured courses in graphic design, branding, and typography. What began as a side curiosity slowly became the work I genuinely love doing.

My process is patient and conversation-led. I'd rather ask a few good questions and design one strong mark than throw fifty rushed ideas at the wall. Every logo here started as a written brief and a sketch, not a template.

When I'm not in Illustrator, I'm reading about design history, studying old logo books, or sketching marks for brands that don't exist yet. The job feels like a craft to me — and I treat every project like it matters, because for the person hiring me, it does.

04 — How We'll Work

A simple, honest process.

01
Brief & conversation
You tell me about your brand — what it does, who it's for, what you want it to feel like. I ask questions until I understand the soul of it, not just the surface.
02
Research & sketching
I study your industry, your competitors, and your audience. Then I sketch — pencil first, always. Most ideas die here, and that's the point.
03
Concepts & refinement
I send you a small number of strong directions — not fifty weak ones. We pick one and refine together until it feels right.
04
Delivery & handover
You receive every file you'll ever need — AI, EPS, PDF, SVG, PNG, JPG — plus a short guide on how to use your new logo across web, print, and social.
